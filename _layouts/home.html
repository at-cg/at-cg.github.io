Chirag Jain

Assistant Professor

Department of Computational and Data Sciences
Indian Institute of Science

CV

Chirag Jain is an Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where he leads the ATCG Lab. His group works at the intersection of computer science and molecular biology, developing efficient algorithms and computational tools to tackle challenging, data-intensive problems in genomics.

His research spans genome sequencing, assembly, pangenomics, and computational genomics, with a focus on algorithms that combine strong theoretical foundations with practical scalability. The goal is to transform increasingly large and complex genomic datasets into actionable insights for biological discovery and human health.

Prior to joining IISc in 2020, Chirag was a postdoctoral fellow at the National Institutes of Health (NIH). He received his Ph.D. from Georgia Tech in 2019 and his B.Tech. in Computer Science from IIT Delhi in 2014. He is a recipient of the IIT Delhi Graduates of the Last Decade Award (2024) and the Georgia Tech College of Computing Dissertation Award (2019).
